PASS if the written document states the author's rules as instructions with their reasons and
caveats, in the author's own names (the Three Doors rule, the Cold Start test, the Slow Lane), so
that a reader who never saw the book could apply them, and the reply says what was left out and
why. FAIL if it is a summary of what the book contains, or drops the named frameworks.
