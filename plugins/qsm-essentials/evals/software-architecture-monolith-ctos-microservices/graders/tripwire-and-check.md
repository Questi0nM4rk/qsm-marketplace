Intent of this eval: an architecture decision should still be correct to challenge a year later; the
record is trusted when it names the cost the teams accept, a concrete number or event that reopens
the decision and a rule turned into a check that fails the build.

PASS when the decision it would record names the cost the teams accept with the choice, one
assumption with a number or event that reopens it (not "when needed"), and at least one rule turned
into a check that fails the build or an alert with a threshold (no imports across modules, no
cycles, intake latency budget at the Monday peak). FAIL when the record has no reopen condition, or
the guard is only a guideline or a review habit.
